v0.22
• Builders Guild automation extended to the Abyss: with the Void Cartography research, automatic roads and outposts in the Abyss (toggles separate from the surface and Underworld ones). Void routes, which cost research points, still have to be built by hand

---

v0.21
• New Divine Exodus Ascension milestone: the Relocation prestige vertex is granted for free at every Ascension, and relocating a city no longer costs anything
• New Defense Spire magic building: automatically strikes a monster 2 hexes away or less, once per second, for 1 Crystal and 1 damage that ignores armor — without spending soldiers
• New Divine Commerce Ascension milestone: automatic selling and buying only require a level 1 Market, every city built receives one for free, and every Market produces twice as much gold per cycle
• New military prestige powers in the Abyss: the Phalanx engages 5 soldiers per attack and only applies a monster's armor once to the whole volley, the Punitive Expedition makes 10% of a garrison strike back the moment a monster attacks it, and Mobile Logistics grants 3 roads to every free Mobile Camp while protecting those that touch it
• New Steel Titan monument: a foundry site paid in Stone, Ore and Mithril that rises as an allied colossus and hunts monsters at your side
• New Great Mithril Forge unique building: raised next to a level 2 Mithril Mine, it spends 1 Mithril per second per Adventurer to grant them +1 damage and +1 armor
• New deep prestige powers: Terrain Knowledge speeds up harvesting by 0.5% per hour spent in each plane, and Expedition Gear reserves 20% of your consumables for the deepest plane reached

v0.20
• New Milestones tab on the Ascension screen: unlocks equivalent divine powers for free based on how many different races have already completed an Ascension
• New Divine Construction and Divine Conquest divine powers: every new outpost automatically gets its basic buildings (Town Hall, production, Palisade, Barracks) and a starting garrison
• New Divine Magic divine power: spells can accumulate up to 5 launch charges free of exhaustion once their cooldown completes, shown as circles; each prestige grants one back
• New Divine Rituals divine power: +1 ritual active in parallel, +25% maximum power, and -20% on the ritual cost scaling
