v0.15
• Horn of Plenty: new divine power (follow-up to Divine Inventory) granting +100% yield to every automatic harvest — stacking with the other yield bonuses — and 20 units of each basic resource every second
• Wrath of God: new divine power (follow-up to Fist of God) granting +100% attack speed to your soldiers, against monsters as well as enemy cities
• Divine Legacy: new divine power, first of a sixth column, which unlocks permanent unique buildings — every Ascension performed opens one slot, retroactively for those already completed
• Eternal Legacy: new divine power (follow-up to Divine Legacy) opening a second permanent unique building slot per Ascension performed
• Prestigious Ascension: new divine power, first of a fifth column, which makes every Ascension grant 1 prestige point per divine essence earned since the beginning of the game — the grant is also paid out when the power is purchased
• Greater Purification: new divine power (follow-up to Prestigious Ascension) through which the Necropolis purifies the Divine Bones it is built on instead of destroying them — their divine essence is harvested for free on construction
• Memory of God: new divine power (follow-up to Eye of God) that reduces the cost growth of repeatable research by 50% and shields them from Ascension — every new cycle starts back at the highest rank ever reached
• Fist of God: new divine power (follow-up to Arm of God) bringing 100 damage down on a hex — to the monster standing there and to each adjacent enemy city. The first strike after each prestige is free
• Void Bridge: new research (follow-up to Void Magic) unlocking a spell that raises the three roads around a void-bordered vertex at once, paid in crystals instead of research points — its cost doubles with every cast
• Star Charts: new research (follow-up to Void Magic) unlocking the Observatory, a monument built on a surface Mountain. Each level lowers the research-point cost multiplier of Void routes, from × 4 down to × 3 once the Observatory is complete
• Divine Necropolis: new research (follow-up to Sacred Reliquary) unlocking the Necropolis, a monument built on unpurified Divine Bones that it consumes — each level raises the Divine Points earned on Ascension by 15%

v0.12
• Arcane Tower: new unique building unlocked on the prestige map (replaces the Withered Grove), which automates construction of Mage Towers and Alchemist Huts, and reduces ritual upkeep cost by 25%
• New automation setting: automatic Mithril Mine construction/upgrades via the Volcanic Forge

v0.11
• Mushroom Wood: new research (follow-up to Fungal Cultivation) allowing Sawmills to harvest Wood from Mushroom Caves, at half speed compared to forests
• Grand Temple: new unique building unlocked by research, which automates Temple construction and grants a stacking prestige bonus per Temple built in the civilization
• New setting in the Automation screen: a global switch to disable all automations with one click, without losing your individual settings

v0.10
• Sales History: new tab in the Market window tracking your recent purchases and sales
• War Herald: new high-tier military research unlocking a free raid on one of your own cities, redirecting all reinforcements toward it
• Extended Builders Guild automation: automatic Town Hall upgrades, Palisade construction, and (with the Underground Cartography research) automatic roads and outposts in the Underworld
• New automation setting: automatic investment in Monuments (Wonder, etc.)
• Resource tooltips: production and consumption breakdown by source

v0.9
• Advanced races: the second row of divine powers now unlocks the Giants and the Garudas. The Garudas, lords of the wind, found their cities by flight — no road needed, up to 3 edges from one of their cities, even across the sea.
• New advanced race: the Mermaids, people of the waves who swarm densely along the coastline — cities can be founded up to 2 edges from the coast, but those not touching Water cap at Town Hall level 2.
